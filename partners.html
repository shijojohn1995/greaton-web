Quality Engineering

4-Stage Production Standard

From deep-source mineral water to precision automated bottling, our production follows strict ISO, ISI, and FSSAI protocols.

01

Source Extraction & Pre-Filtration

Protected source water passes through automated dual-media sand filters and activated carbon absorbers to eliminate particulate sediment and turbidity.

02

7-Stage RO + UV + Ozone

High-pressure reverse osmosis membranes remove dissolved solids. Sub-micron cartridge filters, UV sterilization lamps, and natural ozone ensure biological purity.

03

Automated Zero-Touch Bottling

Cleanroom blow molding, sanitized bottle rinsing, precision volumetric filling, and airtight tamper-evident capping with zero human contact.

04

Batch Lab Testing & Dispatch

Every single production batch undergoes in-house microbiological and physicochemical assays verifying IS 14543 compliance before regional transport.

Partner Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial answers for wholesalers, supermarket retailers, distributors, and event organizers.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for supply?
We accommodate both local retail orders and bulk institutional supply. Standard distribution starts from 50 cases for mixed orders (Drizzle Valley 1L, Unique 500ml/1L/2L, Bisley Soda, and Bisley Carbonated Drinks). Full truckload (FTL) deliveries are available across Kerala and Karnataka.
Which regions are covered by Thirumala Industries' direct fleet?
Operating from our Gundlupet border hub, our dedicated distribution fleet delivers daily across Wayanad (Sulthan Bathery, Kalpetta, Mananthavady), Kozhikode, Malappuram in Kerala, and Gundlupet, Chamarajanagar, Nanjangud, and Mysuru in Karnataka.
Are custom private labeling or event contract bottling services available?
Yes. Thirumala Industries provides certified contract packaging and co-packing for hotel chains, restaurants, resorts, marriage conventions, and corporate events with custom-branded labels adhering to ISI and FSSAI statutory standards.
How is batch water purity tested and verified?
Our facility features an in-house laboratory where trained technicians monitor pH, TDS, turbidity, conductivity, and bacterial counts daily. Every batch meets Bureau of Indian Standards (IS 14543) specifications and undergoes periodic third-party accredited audits.
