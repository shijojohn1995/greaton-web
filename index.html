Our Legacy & Commitment

About Greaton Foods & Food Products

Committed to delivering uncompromising purity and revitalizing refreshment to homes, cafes, and retailers across Kerala.

Welcome to Greaton Foods (Greaton Food Products). Established on 01/07/2025 in Kaniyambetta, Wayanad, we have rapidly emerged as a symbol of pristine quality and trusted distribution in the beverage sector.

From naturally balanced drinking water to invigorating carbonated beverages and vitamin-packed fruit juices, every bottle that carries our name adheres to stringent food safety and hygienic bottling standards.

Our Mission

To provide pure, safe, and revitalizing beverages that promote good health, energize communities, and set higher standards for hydration in Kerala.

Our Vision

To be the most admired household beverage brand in South India, celebrated for continuous innovation, eco-conscious distribution, and unyielding purity.

  • Certified Packaging Quality
  • Rapid Wayanad Logistics
  • Trusted Retailer Network
  • Eco-Friendly PET Bottles
